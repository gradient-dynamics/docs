# Post-processing

Studio turns FluxCore result assets into an interactive engineering view in the
browser.

## Field visualisation

Colour surfaces or volume data by available scalar fields, inspect vector
direction with glyphs, and use consistent ranges when comparing cases. Keep the
colour scale visible in screenshots and reports.

## Derived views

- **Slices** reveal internal flow and thermal structure on defined planes.
- **Streamlines** show the path and topology of the velocity field.
- **Isosurfaces** identify regions at a selected scalar threshold.
- **Probes** report values at a point or along an inspection path.
- **Surface selections** isolate a component or boundary for focused review.

## Engineering quantities

Review named force and moment outputs, pressure and viscous contributions,
mass-flow measures, temperature extrema and heat-transfer metrics created by
the simulation configuration. Integral quantities should be computed from an
explicit, stable surface selection.

## Compare revisions

Use the same camera, field, range, cut location, reference values and output
definition across design variants. Visual differences are meaningful only when
the comparison setup is controlled.

## Export

Download portable field and summary files for archival or specialist analysis.
The project remains the source of truth for the relationship between those
files and their geometry, mesh and simulation configuration.
